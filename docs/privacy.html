RFX RecruitRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

RFX RecruitRush (“the extension”) is a browser extension that helps student athletes fill out college recruiting questionnaires from a profile they save in their own browser. We designed it to be private by default.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. The extension has no account system and no backend server. The recruiting profile you enter (name, contact details, academics, athletics information, parent/guardian contacts, etc.) and your settings are stored only on your device using the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local).

We do not:

  • transmit your profile or any page data to any server we control;
  • use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking;
  • sell or share your data with anyone.

How the extension uses page access

When you click “Fill this form,” use the keyboard shortcut, or use the right-click menu, the extension reads the labels and fields of the page you are on so it can fill them with your saved profile. This happens locally, only on your request, and the contents of those pages are not collected or transmitted.

The extension requests access to all sites because college recruiting questionnaires are hosted on many different university and vendor domains; it needs to work on whichever one you are filling out.

Links to the RFX app

The extension contains links to the RFX recruiting app on the Apple App Store and Google Play. If you click them, you are taken to those stores under their own privacy policies. The links include standard campaign tags so we can understand, in aggregate, how many people install the app from the extension; these do not identify you.

Your control over your data

  • Export your profile to a JSON file anytime from the options page;
  • edit or clear it at any time;
  • remove it entirely by uninstalling the extension.

Children's privacy

The extension stores information locally and does not collect it. If a recruit is under 13, a parent or guardian should set up and manage the profile.

Changes

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.
