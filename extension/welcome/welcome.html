How RecruitRush fills a form

  1. Save your info once — click “Set up my profile” and fill it in.
  2. Open any coach's questionnaire and click the RecruitRush icon → Fill this form (or press Alt+Shift+F).
  3. Review the green-highlighted fields and submit. Repeat for the next school in seconds.
